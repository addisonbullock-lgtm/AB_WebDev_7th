Why I love Pirates

Why I like them

I love Pirates because of how my mind thinks of them.

I would always picture myself on a Galleon pirate ship, feeling the hot summer atmosphere, as I try to balence on the side of the deck, I almost lose my balence as I fear I might fall. I can also imagine going on a island, as many of us perished from the traps in the tresure cave. As me and a rare few crew members go through the small pouring waterfall, as our hats block our heads from getting wet, causing our shoulders to get soaked. As our eyes wander off from a bright yellow light, we were able to adjust our eyes to see the tresure that awaits us.

The plot twist

Before our hands touched the pure shiny gold, along with the greedy mouth watering rubies. Skeletons began to rise out of the gold, drawing their rusted cutlasses to show if they were friend or foe. As me and the rest of the crew drew out our own swords and flintlocks, we began to battle for the gold. As many countless hours of duelling. We managed to take all of the gold to the ship, and spend it all on Gelato.
